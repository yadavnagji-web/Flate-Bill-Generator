Privacy Policy for Sub Meter Bill Generator

Effective Date: September 20, 2026 • Last Updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sub Meter Bill Generator ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and web application service.

1. Data Minimization & Offline-First Architecture

Your Data Stays on Your Device: Sub Meter Bill Generator is built with an offline-first architecture. All tenant names, flat numbers, sub-meter readings, electricity consumption units, and billing records are stored locally on your device (in your browser's private local storage).

We do not operate central database servers storing your personal tenant lists, mobile numbers, or financial collections.

2. Camera & Photo Library Permissions

Sub Meter Bill Generator requests camera or file gallery permissions strictly for the following functional purpose:

  • Electricity Bill Scanning (OCR): To take a photograph or select a PDF / image of your official building electricity utility bill so that numbers, meter units, and bill amounts can be read and entered automatically.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, microphone, or unrelated photo folders.

3. AI Optical Character Recognition (OCR)

When you choose to scan a utility bill, the image of that document is securely processed via server-side OCR solely to extract billing figures (such as total units, bill amount, energy charges, and billing month). This processing is transient and is not used to train models or harvest personal identity records.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The application utilizes Google AdMob to display mobile advertising banners and interstitial units to support free development and hosting:

  • Google AdMob may use device advertising identifiers and standard analytics cookies to serve relevant advertisements in compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies.
  • For more information on how Google manages advertising data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms.

5. WhatsApp & PDF Sharing

When you generate a WhatsApp billing slip or download a building PDF statement:

  • PDF generation is executed directly client-side on your device.
  • When you tap "Share via WhatsApp", the app opens the standard WhatsApp protocol (https://wa.me/) with pre-filled billing breakdown text. We never access your WhatsApp messages or contacts.

6. Data Backup, Export, and Deletion

You have 100% ownership and control of your records:

  • JSON Backup Export: You can export your entire database as a standard JSON file at any time from the app Settings.
  • Instant Data Deletion: You can wipe all stored records immediately by selecting "Clear All Data" in the Settings tab.

7. Children's Privacy

Sub Meter Bill Generator is a utility tool designed for adult property owners, building managers, and tenants. It does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be reflected directly on this page with an updated Effective Date.
